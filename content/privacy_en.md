## 1. Who we are
Logic Mix is a puzzle game for Android published by AKYneX studio ("we"). For privacy questions write to [CONTACT_EMAIL].
The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or location.

## 2. What stays on your device
Your puzzle progress, XP, scores, Arcade marathon, settings, selected categories, language and downloaded content are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. Deleting the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them.

## 3. Anonymous analytics (Firebase Analytics)
Analytics is off by default. It starts only if you switch on "Anonymous analytics" in Settings, and you can switch it off at any time.
When on, Google Firebase Analytics receives events about how the app is used, for example which game category was opened, puzzles started or completed, hints used, Arcade progress, and technical information such as app version, device model, operating system and an app-instance identifier.
We use this to improve puzzles and fix problems. It is not used to identify you.

## 4. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)
Crash reporting follows the same setting as analytics: it is off until you switch on "Anonymous analytics". When on, if the app crashes, Crashlytics receives technical diagnostic data such as the error, device model, operating system version and app version.

## 5. Remote configuration (Firebase Remote Config)
The app fetches settings from Firebase Remote Config, for example how often a hint offer may appear. This request happens whether or not analytics is on, and Google receives technical data needed for it, such as an app-instance identifier and IP address.

## 6. Rewarded ads (Google AdMob and Google UMP)
The app may offer an optional ad in exchange for an extra hint. Ads are requested only after Google's consent flow (User Messaging Platform) allows it. In regions where consent is required, such as the EEA and the UK, a consent form is shown first.
Google AdMob may process your advertising identifier, IP address and device information to show and measure ads. Whether ads are personalized depends on your choice in that form. If a privacy options entry is required, it appears in Settings. Consent for ads is separate from the analytics setting.

## 7. Content updates
The app can download new puzzle content from our server. At the moment this feature is not switched on. If it is switched on, the download request reveals your IP address and app version to the server host. We do not upload your answers or progress.

## 8. Permissions
The app uses the Internet permission (for the services above) and the vibration permission (for haptic feedback). It does not ask for contacts, location, camera, microphone or storage.

## 9. Legal bases (EU/EEA/UK)
Analytics, crash reports and personalized ads are based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings or in the ad privacy options. Remote configuration and non-personalized ad delivery rely on our legitimate interest in operating a working, sustainable game.

## 10. Retention and sharing
Data sent to Google is handled by Google as our service provider under its own terms and retention rules; see the links below. We do not sell your data. Data may be processed in countries outside your own, including the United States, under safeguards used by Google. We have not set our own retention periods beyond those of these providers.

## 11. Your rights
Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. Because we cannot identify you from our data, we may need an app-instance identifier to find anything. You can also contact your local data protection authority. There is no one-click deletion button in the app yet; contact us at [CONTACT_EMAIL].

## 12. Children
Logic Mix is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the age set by local law). Please do not use analytics or ads consent on behalf of a child without a parent's approval.

## 13. Security
We use the protections of the Android platform and Google services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 14. Changes
We may update this policy. The version and date at the top show when it last changed. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
